The important distinction is terracotta rather than orange.

There’s also a nice conceptual connection: the app is about taking action, but not about punishing yourself or maximising productivity. Orange communicates movement and optimism without the aggressive athletic connotations of red or neon green.

One thing I’d avoid

Don’t make the entire UI orange.

Use orange for the things you want the user to notice or act on:

* Start session
* Today’s routine
* Progress rings
* Completed actions
* Streak/progress moments
* Primary CTA
* Logo/icon

Keep the rest almost boringly neutral.
